# Exp - 3 RAILWAY MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

# AIM:
To design and develop a Railway Management System using UML (Unified Modeling Language) by representing the system's functionalities and structure through UML diagrams.
# SRS (Procedure):
1. Identify the requirements: Identify the main functions of the Railway Management System, such as train search, ticket booking, ticket cancellation, passenger details, payment, and train schedule management.

2. Identify the actors: Identify the users who interact with the system, such as Passenger, Admin, Ticket Clerk, and Payment Gateway.

3. Draw the Use Case Diagram: Represent the actors and their interactions with the system, including booking tickets, checking train availability, cancelling tickets, and managing trains.

4. Draw the Class Diagram: Identify classes such as Passenger, Train, Ticket, Reservation, Payment, and Schedule. Define their attributes, methods, and relationships.

5. Draw the Activity Diagram: Represent the workflow of the railway reservation process, from searching for trains to booking tickets and receiving confirmation.

6. Draw the Sequence Diagram: Show the sequence of interactions between the passenger, railway system, payment system, and database during ticket booking.

7.  Prepare the final UML model: Organize all diagrams neatly and provide a clear representation of the system's structure and functionality.
